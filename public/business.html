Our Maize Flour Products

NURIRA offers a range of maize flour products designed to meet different nutritional, commercial, and household needs.
Produced in Rwanda under carefully controlled milling and quality processes,
our flour delivers consistent quality for schools, institutions, retailers, distributors, and families.

Fortified Maize Flour

Vitamin- and mineral-enriched maize flour for nutritious, dependable everyday meals.

Best suited for

School feeding, institutional catering, nutrition programmes, and bulk procurement.

Super White Maize Flour

Finely milled white maize flour with a smooth texture and consistent cooking quality.

Best suited for

Household meals, supermarkets, retail shops, hotels, restaurants, and food-service businesses.

Grain Maize Flour

Traditional-style maize flour with a familiar maize taste and satisfying texture.

Best suited for

Traditional meals, households, local markets, wholesalers, and regional distributors.
