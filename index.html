Our Story

Established in 2020 in Kigali, AGrowth Ltd is a social enterprise dedicated to transforming local maize into premium NURIRA flour. We combine traditional Rwandan agricultural wisdom with modern milling technology to ensure every product meets the highest quality standards for institutional buyers and wholesale partners.
